# Suziee — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Overview
Suziee is a native Android music player featuring premium anime-inspired aesthetics, immersive motion graphics, online music discovery, reliable streaming, offline music playback, playlists, lyrics, and voice-controlled playback.

## 2. Core User Stories & Capabilities
- **Music Search**: Natural search for song title, artist, anime openings/endings, vocal tracks, and Japanese/Hindi/English songs.
- **Playback & Queue**: Continuous background playback with MediaSessionService notification controls, seekbar, next/previous, shuffle, repeat-one, repeat-all, and queue reordering.
- **Offline Mode**: Download permitted music files into app-private storage and play directly without internet connection.
- **Lyrics View**: Live synced timestamped lyrics from LRCLIB that automatically scroll with song playback.
- **Voice Control**: Voice-activated commands to play songs, pause/resume, skip tracks, and navigate.
- **Atmospheric Visuals**: Anime cyberpunk aesthetic with custom adaptive launcher icon, animated waveform visualizer, and themed components.
